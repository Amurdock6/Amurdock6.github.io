Built for real-life outfit planning

Outfit planning that keeps up with real life.

StyleStreak is a smart wardrobe app that helps you plan outfits, track what you actually wear, pack for trips, and figure out what to wear for events when you are not sure where to start. It is designed to help users organize their closet, make faster outfit decisions, and get more value out of the clothes they already own. Our edge is fast wardrobe digitization paired with a simple, polished, and easy-to-use UI/UX. Available on both Android and iOS.

01 Capture your closet

Use live scan, saved photos, or a quick manual add when you need one missing piece in the app fast.

02 Build the look

Filter what you own, preview the fit, and save the main outfit plus a backup before the day gets moving.

03 Pack and repeat smarter

Turn the same wardrobe into trip plans, wear tracking, and stats that show what is actually working.
