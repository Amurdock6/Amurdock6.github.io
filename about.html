about me.

AM / 01

My name is Alex Murdock. I’m a software engineer from Riverton, Utah, I work across full-stack web development, mobile apps, and occasionally automotive focused embedded systems projects. I’ve been hooked on building things basically my entire life. I started building software when I taught myself HTML, CSS, & JS at 15 to build simple websites and from there I’ve constantly been expanding my skill set and tackling more complex projects. These days I always seem to have multiple projects going on at once.

In the rare case I’m not working on one of those projects, I’m probably working on my engine-swapped 35th anniversary 350Z, spending time at a car meet with friends, or traveling. In May 2026, I visited Japan and attended a Super GT race. I got to explore the mecca of automotive culture! Including Daikoku PA, the Nissan Heritage Museum, Nismo omori factory, Hakone Turnpike, etc. It was just about everything a JDM fanboy could want!

01 / 04

I graduated from the University of Utah in May 2026 with a Bachelor of Science in Software Development and a minor in Business, following the Web and Mobile Development track. Professionally, I work as a Software Development Engineer II at the University of Utah. Outside of work, my projects range from websites and mobile apps to reverse engineering and building embedded CAN bus translation software for my 350Z.

These days, I’m deeply integrating AI into my workflows and constantly researching better ways to utilize AI to both build products people actually want to use and create better experiences for users within these products. I’m all in on the AI revolution, and I believe using these tools effectively is the single most important skill a modern software engineer can have. For me, that means making good architecture decisions, giving the tools clear direction, and validating that what they build actually works. I can build more now than I ever could before, and I’ve never had more fun doing it! If you want to hear more about how I am using AI and my thoughts on AI check it out here: My thoughts on AI and the future of software engineering

The project I am most proud of is StyleStreak, the AI-powered wardrobe and outfit planning app I created for my senior capstone project. I came up with the idea, recruited the four-person team, led the product direction and project planning, shaped the architecture, and contributed roughly 70 percent of the current codebase. I built major parts of the Firebase foundation, outfit generation, trip planning and packing, closet intake and filtering, AI integrations and AI benchmarking and Analysis tooling, testing, and CI/CD. StyleStreak won first place at the University of Utah Senior Capstone Demo Day.
