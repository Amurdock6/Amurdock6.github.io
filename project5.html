As a Software Development Engineer II at the University of Utah, my work focuses mainly on integrations, complex flow logic, and more general areas in ServiceNow for campus and healthcare operations. I joined the team as a part-time ServiceNow Developer in October 2022 while completing my Bachelors degree and officially moved into my current role in July 2026, after taking on the same responsibilities and hours in May.

I’ve worked across the platform, from ITOM, ITSM, and HRSD, to CMDB processes, REST/SOAP integrations, testing, operational support, and on-call work.

I proposed and demonstrated an AI-assisted ServiceNow development workflow using VS Code, Git, Codex, and the ServiceNow SDK/Fluent. I built a custom Node.js MCP bridge to provide platform metadata and test-record context, and used project instructions to guide implementation around ServiceNow-specific requirements.

My work included addressing record references, approvals, access controls, and catalog behavior, with backend and browser acceptance checks to evaluate the generated applications.

For a Temporary Privileged Access workflow, I found an 18× productivity improvement compared with developing the same functionality manually. I presented the approach and findings to management and demonstrated the workflow to peers.

I built end-to-end printer provisioning automation by integrating the existing ServiceNow, Infoblox, Cisco ISE, and LRS systems through their APIs. The work included substantially rebuilding an inherited ISE component and coordinating configuration-item creation, IP provisioning, endpoint registration, and printer/DNS operations.

The automation replaces the manual technical fulfillment steps, leaving Client Engineering with request approval. I also built reservation and validation logic, retry handling, status tracking, and remediation paths for collisions, timeouts, and partial failures.
