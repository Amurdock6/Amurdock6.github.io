# Portfolio content workflow

Alex wants human-authored website content. Any AI-generated wording requires Alex’s explicit review and approval before it is added to the website.

- Suggest topics or projects in conversation. Alex chooses a topic and writes the first draft.
- Do not draft, rewrite, polish, or enhance that content unless Alex specifically asks. Wait for direction after receiving a draft.
- Keep any requested AI edits as reviewable suggestions outside the served website until Alex approves the exact wording.
- This applies to blogs, case studies, project descriptions, biographies, headings, captions, and metadata. A general request to update the portfolio or access to career evidence does not approve generated copy.
- Keep withdrawn or unapproved drafts outside this repository’s served directory. Do not expose them as hidden pages, unlinked HTML files, or site assets.
- Preserve the existing minimalist styling, image-driven project boxes, StyleStreak highlight, and the original landing-page typewriter effect and headline.
- Technical fixes may proceed within the authorized scope while preserving editorial copy. Do not commit, push, or publish unapproved content.

The content that existed before this session was restored as Alex’s existing baseline; its authorship was not independently audited.
